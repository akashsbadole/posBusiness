## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Access the System
1. Navigate to the POSBiz login page
2. Choose a demo account from the categories above
3. Click on the account card to auto-fill credentials
4. Click "Sign In" to access the dashboard

### 2. Explore Features
- **Dashboard:** View business analytics and metrics
- **Orders:** Create and manage customer orders
- **Menu:** Manage products, services, and pricing
- **Customers:** Handle customer data and loyalty programs
- **Inventory:** Track stock levels and manage supplies
- **Staff:** Manage employee roles and permissions
- **Settings:** Configure business-specific options

### 3. Test Industry-Specific Features
- **Restaurants:** Table management, kitchen orders
- **Retail:** Product catalog, barcode scanning
- **Services:** Appointment booking, scheduling
- **Healthcare:** Patient management, prescriptions
- **Hospitality:** Room management, service integration
